Amtrak Student Discount: How to Get 15% Off Your Train Ticket

Students aged 17–24 save 15% with promo code V814. Here's who qualifies, how to apply it, and how to stack it for even bigger savings.

Yes, Amtrak has a student discount — 15% off most routes, available year-round, no blackout dates. If you're between 17 and 24 and haven't been using it, you've been leaving money on the table every trip.

Here's everything you need: who qualifies, how to apply the discount online and in the app, which routes are excluded, and — the part most guides skip — how to stack it with other programs to cut your fare even further.

National student promo code
V814
Enter this at checkout on the Amtrak app. On the website, select "Student" as your passenger type and the discount applies automatically.

Who qualifies for the Amtrak student discount?

The national Amtrak student discount has three requirements:

  • Age: 17–24 years old (some regional programs extend this to 13–25 — more on those below)
  • Enrollment: Currently enrolled at an accredited high school, college, or university
  • Booking window: Tickets must be purchased at least one day in advance — no same-day bookings

You'll need to show a valid student ID to the conductor when you board. Digital copies on your phone are generally accepted, but carry the physical card to be safe. If you can't prove student status when asked, you may be charged the difference to the full adult fare on the spot.

International students

F-1 and J-1 visa holders studying at US institutions are fully eligible. If asked for proof of enrollment, show your I-20 form (F-1) or DS-2019 (J-1) alongside your student ID. The same age requirements apply.

Graduate students within the 17–24 age range qualify — master's, doctoral, and professional degree students are all eligible. Once you turn 25, the national discount no longer applies, though some state-level programs have higher age limits.

How to apply the discount: step by step

There are two ways to book — the website and the app. Both work, but the steps differ slightly.

On the Amtrak website (amtrak.com)

1
Go to amtrak.com and enter your route and dates

Use the main journey planner. Enter your origin, destination, and travel date as normal.

2
Change passenger type to "Student"

In the passengers field, switch from "Adult" to "Student". The 15% discount will be applied automatically to eligible fares — no code needed on the website.

3
Select your fare and proceed to checkout

Student fares show alongside standard fares. Pick your preferred fare type (Value, Flexible, etc.) and continue. The discounted price will be reflected at checkout.

4
Confirm the discount has applied before paying

Double-check the fare breakdown on the payment screen. The student discount should be listed as a line item. If it isn't showing, try entering code V814 manually in the promo code field.

On the Amtrak app

1
Search for your trip as normal

Enter origin, destination, and date in the app's journey planner.

2
Open the "Add Coupon" field at checkout

On the booking screen, tap "Add Coupon" and enter V814. Unlike the website, the app requires manual code entry — it won't apply automatically.

3
Verify the price updates before completing payment

The fare total should drop by 15% once the code is accepted. If V814 isn't working on your route, the route may be excluded (see below) or the discount may be unavailable for your travel dates.

Fares vary significantly by travel date and how far in advance you book — the figures above reflect typical Value fare pricing. The student discount applies on top of whatever the current base fare is, so booking earlier (when fares are lower) gives you the biggest absolute saving.

Note: the discount is coach only on most routes. It applies to Acela Business class but not to sleeping car accommodations. If you're booking a roomette or bedroom, the full sleeper fare applies.

Regional student discounts that beat 15%

This is the section most guides skip entirely. Several Amtrak state-funded corridors have their own student programs with wider age ranges and, in some cases, higher discounts than the national 15%.

The Midwest program is particularly useful because it covers routes explicitly excluded from the national V814 discount — so if you're riding the Wolverine or Hiawatha, V307 is your code, not V814.

The New York program extends eligibility down to age 13, which means high school students travelling within New York State are covered. The blackout dates for regional programs do differ from the national discount — check the specific program page on Amtrak.com for your travel dates.

How to stack discounts for maximum savings

The student discount can't be combined with other promo codes, but there are several legitimate ways to reduce your effective fare further.

Always do this
Join Amtrak Guest Rewards first
Points still accrue on discounted tickets. Sign up before you book — the points from long-distance student fares add up fast and can offset future travel costs.
For group travel
Student fare + Share Fare
Travelling with 2–7 friends? Amtrak's Share Fare gives companions up to 50% off when one person buys a full ticket. Combine with student pricing for the group discount route.
Book earlier
Student discount on a Saver fare
Book well in advance to hit the lowest Saver fare tier, then apply the student discount on top. The absolute saving is higher when the base fare is lower. Non-refundable, so fix your dates first.
Midwest / NY only
Multi-ride passes with student pricing
Some excluded corridor routes offer 10-ride passes with built-in student discounts — better value if you commute regularly between fixed points. Check the route's dedicated page.

One thing to be clear on: the student discount cannot be used for bookings made with Amtrak Guest Rewards points, nor for Points & Cash redemptions. It applies to cash fares only.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book same-day tickets with the student discount?

No. The discount requires booking at least one day in advance. If you need to travel same-day, you'll pay the full adult fare.

What if the conductor asks for ID and I don't have it?

You may be charged the difference between the student fare and the full adult fare on the spot. Always bring your student ID — a digital copy on your phone is usually fine, but physical is safer.

Can I use it on the Acela?

Yes, but only for Coach and Acela Business class. It doesn't apply to Acela First Class or sleeping cars on any train.

Does it work on all long-distance Amtrak trains?

Yes — the national discount applies to all long-distance routes not listed in the excluded section above. The California Zephyr, Empire Builder, Coast Starlight, City of New Orleans, Lake Shore Limited, and Auto Train all qualify for V814.

Can I combine the student discount with a sale fare?

You can use the student discount on a Saver fare (Amtrak's cheapest fare tier) — those are separate things. What you can't do is stack two promo codes, or use the student discount alongside a different percentage-off promotion.

My code V814 isn't working — what do I do?

First, check you're not on an excluded route (see the list above). Second, confirm you're booking at least one day out. Third, try on the website instead of the app — the passenger type selector sometimes works when the code field doesn't. If none of that works, call Amtrak customer service; occasionally the discount inventory for a specific departure sells out.
