Amtrak California Zephyr: The Complete 2025 Guide

Chicago to San Francisco across the Rockies and Sierra Nevada — tickets, sleeper cars, scenic highlights, and how to book.

The California Zephyr is widely considered Amtrak's most scenic long-distance route — a 51-hour journey that crosses eight states, climbs through the Colorado Rockies, and descends into California via the Sierra Nevada. If you're going to take one Amtrak trip in your life, make it this one.

Running daily between Chicago Union Station and Emeryville (San Francisco Bay Area), the Zephyr covers 2,438 miles through landscapes that will make even the most seasoned traveler put down their phone.

Quick note on timing

The California Zephyr runs daily in both directions. Westbound departs Chicago at 2:00 PM. Eastbound departs Emeryville at 9:10 AM. Scheduled journey time is 51 hours 20 minutes westbound — budget 2–4 extra hours for delays.

Tickets: coach vs sleeper vs roomette

Coach (from ~$89)

Coach seats are large, reclining, and comfortable for shorter legs. For the full 51-hour trip, coach is manageable. You get access to the Sightseer Lounge regardless of ticket class.

Roomette (from ~$250)

The roomette is the sweet spot — a private room for two with fold-down beds, a large window, and meals included in the dining car. Highly recommended for overnight segments.

How to book and get the best price

  • Book 11–14 months ahead for the lowest coach fares.
  • Travel midweek — Tuesday and Wednesday are 15–25% cheaper than weekends.
  • Use Amtrak Guest Rewards — points accumulate fast on long-distance routes.
  • Amtrak student discount gives 15% off for verified students.

The best scenic stretches

  • Moffat Tunnel to Glenwood Canyon (Day 2, ~10 AM–1 PM): The most dramatic stretch. Glenwood Canyon has 1,700-foot walls with the Colorado River below.
  • Truckee to Sacramento via Sierra Nevada (Day 3 morning): Donner Pass, pine forests, and the descent into the Central Valley at sunrise.

Practical tips

  • Bring an eye mask and earplugs even in a sleeper.
  • Download offline maps and entertainment — cellular dead zones are long in Nevada and Colorado.
  • Power outlets are at every coach seat and in all sleeper rooms.
  • The Sightseer Lounge fills up fast — stake a spot by 9:30 AM westbound through Colorado.

Frequently asked questions

Is the California Zephyr worth it?

Yes — unambiguously. The Glenwood Canyon section rivals anything in the Swiss Alps. Even if you're not a train person, it's worth doing once.

How often is it late?

Frequently. Average delay on the full westbound run is around 3 hours. Don't book a tight connection at Emeryville.

Do I need a rail pass?

No — Amtrak point-to-point tickets are almost always cheaper than the USA Rail Pass for a single route.
